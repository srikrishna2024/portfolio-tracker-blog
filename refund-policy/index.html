Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2026

No Refunds

All purchases of Personal Portfolio Tracker are final. We do not offer refunds, exchanges, or credits under any circumstances once a license key has been issued.

Why We Have This Policy

Personal Portfolio Tracker is a digital software product. Once a license key is delivered, it grants immediate access to the full app. Unlike a physical product, digital software cannot be returned. The license key can only be revoked in cases of terms violations such as sharing the key, reselling the app, or reverse engineering — not for a change of mind or dissatisfaction.

Before You Buy

We want you to be confident before purchasing. We encourage you to:

  • Read the FAQ thoroughly
  • Check the System Requirements to confirm your machine is compatible
  • Contact us with any questions before purchasing. We are happy to answer them.

Setup Support

If you are having difficulty setting up the app after purchase, please contact us before concluding that it does not work. Most setup issues can be resolved quickly. We will make every effort to help you get the app running on your machine.

Exceptions

No exceptions are made to this policy. This includes but is not limited to: change of mind, inability to meet system requirements after purchase, accidental purchase, or dissatisfaction with features.
