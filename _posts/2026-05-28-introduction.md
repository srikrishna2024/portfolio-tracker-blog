Most financial apps make a quiet bargain with you: convenience in exchange for your data. Your portfolio lives on someone else's server, and you have no real way to verify what happens to it.

Personal Portfolio Tracker is built on the opposite assumption. Every transaction, every goal, every number you enter stays on your own computer. Nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly choose to enable AI features.

---

## Who This Is For

The app is built for the Indian investor who:

- Holds mutual funds across CAMS or MF Central — equity, debt, ELSS, hybrid
- Also has EPF, NPS, PPF, and Superannuation that don't appear in those statements
- Has real financial goals — retirement, children's education, a house — and wants to track them rigorously
- Has used CAGR calculators and found them insufficient
- Wants clarity without depending on a distributor or advisor who earns from product sales

If you've been managing your portfolio in a spreadsheet and wished it could tell you more, this is what that spreadsheet would have become.

---

## The Core Idea

The app runs entirely on your own computer. There is no web server, no account to create, no subscription that can lapse, and no company that can lose or sell your data. Your financial history is stored in a local database on your machine — backed up when you choose, moved to a new machine whenever you want.

The AI features (powered by Claude by Anthropic) are strictly optional. If you enable them, the app can answer questions about your portfolio and generate recommendations. If you leave them off, no data ever leaves your machine.

---

## Beyond the CAGR Calculator

Most fund apps answer one question: *"What has this fund returned?"*

This app answers a different question: *"Is this fund doing the job I need it to do — and am I on track to reach my goal?"*

That shift — from fund-centric to goal-centric — is what makes it useful for actual financial planning rather than just performance tracking.

When you open your dashboard, you see a Portfolio Health Score. It reflects not just returns, but whether your money is mapped to specific goals, whether those goals are on track, and whether your funds are reliable enough to be counted on.

---

## What It Covers

- **Import your full portfolio** — mutual funds from CAMS or MF Central, plus EPF, NPS, PPF, and Superannuation from their respective statements. No manual re-entry.
- **Track everything in one place** — live values, returns, and performance vs the benchmark across all instruments.
- **Plan around goals** — create goals, map your funds to them, and see whether you're on track.
- **Analyse your funds** — compare performance, identify underperformers, and verify whether a fund is actually doing what its category label claims.
- **Manage tax** — LTCG and STCG calculations, tax harvesting opportunities, and scenario planning.
- **Get alerts** — when a goal drifts, when a fund underperforms, when a tax deadline approaches.
