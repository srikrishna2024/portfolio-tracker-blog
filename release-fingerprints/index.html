Release Fingerprints

Every release of Personal Portfolio Tracker ships with a SHA256 fingerprint — a unique code that proves the files you received are exactly the files that were published. This page is the public record.

How to verify your copy

  1. Open the software and go to Admin → Privacy Audit
  2. Scroll to Section 4: Code Integrity
  3. The software computes the fingerprint of the files on your machine and shows it
  4. Compare it to the fingerprint listed here for your version

If the fingerprints match, your copy is untampered. If they do not match, do not use that copy — re-download from the original link in your purchase email.

This check runs entirely offline. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Note on the release page fingerprint: The release notes page for each version shows a separate fingerprint for the compiled distribution zip. That value will differ from the one listed here — the two fingerprints are computed from different things (source manifest vs compiled output). The fingerprint on this page is the one to compare against what Admin → Privacy Audit shows inside the app.

Tamper-proof release evidence

Every release of Personal Portfolio Tracker is cryptographically recorded at the moment of publication in multiple independent systems. This creates tamper-proof evidence of exactly what was released, when, and to whom.

If anyone claims the software was modified after distribution, that claim can be verified or disproved conclusively. The fingerprint on this page, the fingerprint in the buyer's purchase email, and the fingerprint the software computes on the buyer's machine will all agree if the copy is genuine. Any mismatch is detectable and constitutes evidence of tampering.

This record is available to any party — buyer, developer, or legal authority — and cannot be altered after publication.
