What You Can Do on Day 1

Setup is done. Now what? Here is exactly what your first session looks like and what you will know by the end of it.

Your first session checklist

This is a realistic picture of what you can accomplish in your first 60 to 90 minutes with the software. You do not need to do everything at once. Each step adds more clarity.

1

Import your mutual fund statement

10 minutes

Download your Consolidated Account Statement from CAMS (camsonline.com) or MF Central (mfcentral.com) and upload it. The software reads all your transactions automatically.

You will see:

Every fund you hold with current value, total invested, gain or loss, and XIRR since your first purchase.

2

Check your overall XIRR vs benchmark

2 minutes

Go to My Portfolio → Portfolio Analysis. The annual performance chart shows your portfolio return each year against the NIFTY 50 TRI benchmark.

You will know:

Whether your active fund selection has added value above a simple index fund, year by year, not just as a cumulative number.

3

Create your first goal

5 minutes

Go to Goal Planning → Create a Goal. Start with the goal you care most about, whether that is retirement, your child's education, or a house purchase. Describe it in plain English and fill in the target amount and timeline.

You will know:

Whether you are on track. The software calculates the required monthly SIP and compares it to what you are currently investing.

4

Assign your funds to the goal

5 minutes

Go to Goal Planning → Goal Fund Manager. Tell the software which of your funds is working towards which goal. You can split a fund across multiple goals by percentage.

You will know:

Your portfolio health score. This is the number that tells you, in one figure, whether your current portfolio is on track to meet your goals.

5

Check your fund reliability scores

5 minutes

Go to Goal Planning → Goal Diagnostics. Look at the K4 score for your goal. This measures how consistently your funds track their benchmark. A high K4 means your funds are unpredictable. A low K4 means you can count on them.

You will know:

Whether the funds in your goal are reliable trackers or whether they are introducing unnecessary uncertainty into your plan.

6

Import your EPF or NPS (if you have them)

10 minutes

Go to Data Import → Debt Import. Upload your EPF passbook from the UMANG app, your NPS statement from NSDL, or your PPF statement. Each has its own tab with instructions.

You will know:

Your real equity-to-debt ratio. For most salaried investors, this is significantly different from what their mutual fund portfolio alone suggests.

7

Take your first backup

2 minutes

Go to Admin → Backup and click Create Backup. Copy the file to Google Drive or OneDrive. This takes 2 minutes and protects everything you just set up.

You will have:

A complete recoverable copy of your portfolio history that you can restore to any machine in minutes.

What you will know after Day 1

📊

Actual XIRR

Your real return, calculated correctly, including all purchases and redemptions at their actual dates.

🎯

Goal gap

Exactly how much you are short on your most important goal and what monthly SIP is needed to close it.

⚖️

Real allocation

Your actual equity-to-debt split including EPF, NPS, and PPF, not just mutual funds.

🏥

Health score

A single number that tells you whether your overall portfolio is on track to meet your goals.

What comes next

After Day 1, the software becomes a quarterly review tool. Every April, run the Annual Review, check K4 and K8 scores, flag any underperforming funds, and adjust SIPs if needed. The daily routine is just a 2-minute data refresh and a look at your dashboard.

Most users find that the first session changes how they think about their portfolio. Numbers that felt abstract become concrete goals with clear gaps and clear actions.
