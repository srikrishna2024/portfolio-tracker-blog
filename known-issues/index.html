How to report a new issue

If your issue is not listed here:

  1. Open the software and go to Admin → Report a Bug
  2. Fill in the form — it automatically collects your app version, OS, and error log
  3. Download the generated report and email it with any screenshots to support@personalfinanceapp.in

Critical bugs are responded to the same day. Non-critical bugs are fixed in the next scheduled release.
