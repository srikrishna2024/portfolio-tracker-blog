Release Fingerprints

Every release of Personal Portfolio Tracker ships with a SHA256 fingerprint — a unique code that proves the files you received are exactly the files that were published. This page is the public record.

How to verify your copy

  1. Open the software and go to Admin → Privacy Audit
  2. Scroll to Section 4: Code Integrity
  3. The software computes the fingerprint of the files on your machine and shows it
  4. Compare it to the fingerprint listed here for your version

If the fingerprints match, your copy is untampered. If they do not match, do not use that copy — re-download from the original link in your purchase email.

This check runs entirely offline. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Why this page exists

Every release of Personal Portfolio Tracker is permanently recorded in multiple independent systems at the moment of publication. The fingerprint on this page, the fingerprint in your purchase email, and the fingerprint your software computes locally will all match if your copy is genuine.

If they do not match, do not use that copy. Re-download from the original link in your purchase email and contact us.

This page is published automatically at the time of each release and cannot be retroactively altered without detection. Any claim that the software was modified after distribution can be verified against these records, providing a clear basis for legal protection if needed.
