Why Personal Portfolio Tracker?

There are dozens of portfolio apps. Here is why this one is different and why it was built.

The problem with most portfolio tools

Most tools answer one question: what has this fund returned?

That is a useful number. But it does not tell you whether you are on track for retirement. It does not tell you whether your daughter's education goal is fully funded. It does not tell you whether the fund you are holding is actually reliable enough to count on, or whether it is generating returns through luck and concentration rather than consistent execution.

The shift from tracking what happened to understanding whether you are on track requires a completely different kind of tool.

What CAMS and MF Central do not show you

CAMS and MF Central are transaction records. They tell you what you bought, when, and at what price. That is their job and they do it well.

What they do not show you:

✗
Goal progress

No concept of what the money is for. All funds treated as one undifferentiated portfolio.

✗
EPF, NPS, PPF

Your debt instruments are invisible. You cannot see your real debt-to-equity ratio or your actual retirement picture.

✗
Fund reliability

A fund that returned 18% last year might have done it through concentrated bets. No way to tell if it will repeat.

✗
Whether your SIP is enough

You know how much you invest. You do not know if it is sufficient to reach your goal on time.

What about wealth managers and advisors?

A good fee-only advisor is genuinely useful. The problem is that most investors interact with distributors, not advisors. Distributors earn a trail commission on the products they recommend. Their interest is not always aligned with yours.

Even with a good advisor, you are dependent on someone else to understand your own portfolio. Personal Portfolio Tracker gives you the tools to understand it yourself, ask better questions, and make more informed decisions. It is not a replacement for advice. It is what you need to be an informed participant in your own financial planning.

Why local and private

Financial data is among the most sensitive data you hold. It reveals your income, your savings rate, your goals, your employer, your life stage, and your risk tolerance. The case for keeping it on your own machine is simple: a server that does not exist cannot be breached. Data that is never uploaded cannot be sold.
