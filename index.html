How it works

1

Import your statements

Upload your CAMS or MF Central PDF. Add EPF, NPS, PPF, and Superannuation statements too. Everything lands in one place with no manual entry.

2

Map funds to your goals

Create goals like retirement, education, or a house purchase and link your investments to each one. The app tracks progress towards every goal separately.

3

Track, analyse, and act

See your health score, spot underperformers, check your tax position, and get alerts when something needs attention. Review once a quarter or once a year.

What you get

🎯

Goal-based tracking

Map your investments to specific goals. See exactly how much you need, how much you have, and whether your monthly SIP is on track.

📥

Full portfolio import

Import mutual funds from CAMS or MF Central, plus EPF, NPS, PPF, and Superannuation. All instruments in one view.

📊

Portfolio health score

A single score that reflects goal progress, fund reliability, and concentration risk. Not just returns.

🔬

Fund analysis

Compare funds, check rolling returns, measure downside risk, and verify whether a fund actually behaves the way its category label suggests.

💰

Tax calculations

Accurate LTCG and STCG using the FIFO method. Identify harvesting opportunities to reduce next year's tax bill.

🔔

Alerts and monitoring

Automated alerts for goal drift, fund underperformance, concentration risk, and tax deadlines so nothing slips through.

🏖️

Retirement planning

Project your retirement corpus, calculate the SIP you need, and simulate how long your savings will last in retirement.

💬

Ask your portfolio

Type a question in plain English and get an answer backed by your actual data. Which fund has the best returns? Am I on track for retirement? Just ask.

🧠

Behaviour analytics

See patterns in your own investing behaviour. SIP consistency, how you responded to past market crashes, timing quality, and coaching notes.

🛡️

Complete privacy

Your data is stored on your own computer. The app includes a built-in privacy audit that gives you live proof. No cloud, no tracking.

💥

Crash simulator

Stress-test your portfolio against past market crashes. See how your current corpus would have fared and whether your goals would have survived a bad sequence of returns.

🪣

Bucket strategy simulator

Model retirement withdrawals across three buckets with different risk levels. See how long each lasts, when refills happen, and whether your corpus survives to your target age.
