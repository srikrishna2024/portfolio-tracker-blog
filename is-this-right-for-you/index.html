Is This Right for You?

Personal Portfolio Tracker is not for everyone. This page is an honest guide to help you decide before you buy.

You will get the most out of this if you

✓

Hold mutual funds through CAMS or MF Central and want to track them against specific life goals, not just as a single portfolio

✓

Have EPF, NPS, PPF, or Superannuation and want to see your complete financial picture in one place

✓

Are planning for retirement and want real numbers: corpus required, monthly SIP needed, projected shortfall

✓

Want to know whether your funds are actually reliable trackers of their benchmarks, not just whether they returned well last year

✓

Care about where your financial data lives and want it on your own machine, not on a company's server

✓

Are comfortable installing software and following a setup guide (20 minutes, step-by-step)

✓

Want to make informed decisions about your own portfolio without depending entirely on a distributor or advisor

What this software is not

Being clear about this helps you get the right value from it.

✗

Not a fund recommendation engine. The software analyses the funds you already hold. It does not tell you what to buy next. Its job is to help you manage what you have with discipline, not chase what someone else is buying.

✗

Not a trading tool. This is for long-term investors who believe in minimal churn. If you are looking for signals to buy and sell frequently, this is not that software.

✗

Not a mobile app. This is desktop software for quarterly or annual deep review, not a daily balance-checking app. The real work of investing is not watching prices. It is making sure your goals are on track.

The investing philosophy behind this software

The best investment strategy for most people is simple: a focused portfolio of 3 to 5 funds, consistent SIPs tied to real goals, and the discipline to stay the course when markets get noisy.

Most investors underperform not because they picked the wrong funds, but because they churned too often, chased last year's winners, panicked in corrections, or lost sight of why they were investing in the first place.

Personal Portfolio Tracker is built around this idea. It shows you whether your goals are on track, whether your funds are doing their job reliably, and whether your current SIP is sufficient. It helps you resist FOMO by showing you what actually matters: goal progress, not quarterly rankings.

The best financial decision most people can make is to earn more by investing in their career, keep their portfolio simple, and stay consistent. This software is the tool that makes staying consistent easier.

The setup commitment

Setup takes about 20 minutes on a first install. You will need to:

  1. Install Python 3.14 from python.org (free, 5 minutes)
  2. Install PostgreSQL from postgresql.org (free, 5 minutes)
  3. Run the Setup Wizard inside the software (guided, 5 minutes)
  4. Download fund data from AMFI (automatic, 10 minutes waiting)

After that, the daily routine is: start the software, run a 2-minute data refresh, and check your dashboard. The heavy setup happens once.

If you are uncertain whether setup will work on your machine, write to me before purchasing. I will tell you honestly whether your setup is likely to work smoothly.

What you get on day one

After completing setup and importing your CAMS or MF Central statement, you will immediately see:

  • All your fund holdings with current value, invested amount, and XIRR
  • Your overall portfolio XIRR vs the NIFTY 50 TRI benchmark
  • A goal health score once you create your first goal
